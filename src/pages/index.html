The enterprise AI problem

AI adoption is growing.
Oversight needs to keep up.

When teams choose separate assistants, models and workflows, each brings another set of permissions, costs and records. Turning those experiments into dependable services becomes an organisation-wide challenge.

  1. 01

    Disconnected tools

    Knowledge and workflows stay in silos. Teams repeat integration work instead of sharing capabilities.

  2. 02

    Uneven controls

    Access, data handling and model budgets vary between applications, making consistent oversight harder.

  3. 03

    Incomplete evidence

    Scattered records make it difficult to connect an answer to its source, cost and approval history.

Our answer is not one more tool. It is the governed layer underneath all of them.

Governance

Controls throughout execution.

Shared policies connect each request to its permissions, execution history and evidence.

01 / Before

Check access and policy

Resolve identity, authorise model access and check budgets before a request runs.

02 / During

Apply controls as work runs

Enforce guardrails and data policies, with human approval gates for consequential actions. Monitor runs and costs through AIRA Signal.

03 / After

Keep reviewable evidence

Record runs, approvals and policy decisions in a hash-chained audit log for investigation and export.

Fourteen use cases, labelled honestly.

In catalog01 · Banking · KYC/AML

Customer Onboarding Review

Problem
Analysts read each applicant's ID, proof of address and supporting documents against a KYC/AML rulebook: slow, repetitive, high-stakes.
In AIRA
A Document AI Solution ingests and OCRs the files, retrieves the relevant rules from your knowledge base and returns per-document checks, missing or expired items, a risk rating and a recommendation.
Controls
A typed form, rules grounded in your own knowledge base, optional analyst approval, the full run on the record.
In catalog02 · Banking · Retail lending

Credit Intelligence

Problem
Underwriters rebuild income and spending pictures from bank statements by hand.
In AIRA
Upload a statement and receive an underwriting-ready assessment: income stability, monthly cash flow, debt exposure and a recommendation, optionally grounded in your lending policy.
Controls
Local models keep statements inside the perimeter. PII guardrails before any cloud call. Cost per run is capped.
In catalog03 · Banking · Corporate credit

Intelligent Credit Review

Problem
Corporate reviews combine financials, facility requests and policy limits across many documents.
In AIRA
Financial statements go in; the relevant credit policy is retrieved; the assessment surfaces exceptions and the approval level required, citing the clause. A credit memo comes out.
Controls
Policy citations, an approval gate before sharing, versioned publishing so approved logic is the logic that runs.
In catalog04 · Banking · Corporate onboarding

Bulk Client Document Review

Problem
An onboarding pack arrives as dozens of mixed documents that someone must identify and check.
In AIRA
One job over the whole pack identifies each document, checks it against the onboarding standard and returns a consolidated result.
Controls
Office conversion and OCR at ingest, per-document results, full traceability for each file.
In catalog05 · Banking · Data quality

Address Intelligence

Problem
Free-text addresses in legacy files block payments formatting, screening and migrations.
In AIRA
A Batch AI Solution: upload a CSV and get standardised, structured address fields row by row, with rollups and a clean CSV to download.
Controls
Row-level results for review, run history that re-renders exactly, cost visible per run.
In catalog06 · All regulated sectors

Policy & regulation assistant

Problem
Staff cannot find the current policy, so they ask colleagues or guess.
In AIRA
Curated knowledge collections (policy library, KYC/AML rules, lending policy, compliance evidence) answerable in Hub Ask, citing the source passage.
Controls
Collection-level access, a grounding gate that abstains rather than guesses, every question on the record.
In catalog07 · Cross-industry · HR

Talent Match

Problem
Recruiters screen CVs inconsistently against a job description.
In AIRA
Upload candidate CVs and the job description, optionally with a candidate knowledge base, and receive a scored shortlist with reasons.
Controls
Reviewable reasons, the human decision retained, PII guardrails, entitlement-limited access.
Demo estate08 · Banking · Financial crime

Fraud transaction screening

Problem
Alert queues grow faster than investigators can clear them.
In AIRA
A transaction summary in; a risk score, matched signals and a verdict (clean, review or block) out. The demo uses a simple heuristic that you replace with your own rules during a pilot.
Controls
A deterministic setting, a typed output, and approval on review and block verdicts before any action.
Demo estate09 · Cross-industry · Service

Customer support triage

Problem
Requests arrive in chat channels with no routing or first draft.
In AIRA
An @support mention in Slack triggers a triage agent that classifies the issue, retrieves runbook content and drafts an in-thread reply, held for sign-off.
Controls
Trigger and reply pass only through Runtime's channel door. Approval on the record. Delivery retried.
Demo estate10 · Cross-industry · HR

Batch résumé triage

Problem
Hundreds of applications for one role are screened by hand.
In AIRA
A batch job takes a ZIP of résumés and a role profile, then summarises and ranks every candidate in one run for recruiter review.
Controls
Human decision retained, PII guardrails, a per-run cost cap.
Studio templates11 · Cross-industry

Business correspondence & summaries

Problem
Staff draft the same memos, status reports and customer emails every week.
In AIRA
Studio's templates (meeting summary, customer email, business memo, status report) become governed assistants in Hub.
Controls
Models chosen by policy. DLP before and after the completion. Usage and spend attributed per user.
Pattern12 · Cross-industry · Procurement

Contract renewal watch

Problem
Auto-renewal and notice clauses are missed across hundreds of supplier contracts.
In AIRA
A scheduled Monitor AI Solution reads the contract repository and flags clauses approaching their notice dates; owners get a Work item and an alert.
Controls
Scheduled trigger through Runtime, read-only sources, alerts only through the channel door.
Pattern13 · Government

Citizen correspondence triage

Problem
Agencies receive large volumes of letters and emails that need routing and a compliant reply.
In AIRA
An email trigger starts a workflow that classifies each item, finds the responsible unit and drafts a reply grounded in published policy, held for an officer's approval.
Controls
Air-gapped with local models, approval before sending, a complete trail for freedom-of-information requests.
Pattern14 · All regulated sectors

Regulatory change monitoring

Problem
Compliance teams track new circulars by hand and map them to internal policy late.
In AIRA
A Monitor AI Solution ingests new publications, compares them with your policy library and opens a Work item listing the affected policies.
Controls
Cited comparisons, the policy owner's decision on the record, evidence exportable to audit.

Catalog and demo-estate items run in the AIRA demo estate with sample data; adapting them to your rules, documents and systems is part of a pilot. Patterns are illustrative designs built from standard AIRA capabilities. Outcomes are measured against each pilot's own baseline.

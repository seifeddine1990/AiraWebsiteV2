In catalog01 · Banking · Retail & SME lending

Credit Review Assistant

Scope
Reviews retail and SME credit applications using customer data, income, liabilities, repayment capacity, and bureau information.
In AIRA
Checks applications against lending policies and highlights exceptions, missing information, and risk factors.
Outcome
Produces a structured credit assessment with key findings and a recommendation for further review.
In catalog02 · Banking · Corporate credit

Corporate Credit Proposal Assistant

Scope
Analyzes corporate credit proposals, financial statements, facilities, collateral, cash flow, and group exposure.
In AIRA
Highlights financial trends, policy deviations, concentration risks, covenants, and missing documents.
Outcome
Generates an executive credit summary suitable for relationship managers, credit analysts, and credit committees.
Pattern03 · Banking · Financial crime

AML & Sanctions Assistant

Scope
Supports investigation of AML alerts, sanctions matches, PEP screening, and suspicious transaction cases.
In AIRA
Summarizes customer profiles, transactions, screening results, and related risk indicators in one view.
Outcome
Helps analysts identify false positives, escalation points, and information required for case review.
In catalog04 · Banking · Customer due diligence

KYC / CDD Assistant

Scope
Reviews customer onboarding and periodic-review documents for individuals and businesses.
In AIRA
Extracts identity, ownership, address, business activity, UBO, and source-of-funds information from submitted documents.
Outcome
Identifies missing, expired, inconsistent, or higher-risk information before KYC approval.
Demo estate05 · Cross-industry · Service

Customer Service Assistant

Scope
Assists branch, contact-center, and digital-service employees with customer questions and service requests.
In AIRA
Uses approved product, policy, fee, process, and FAQ information to generate consistent responses.
Outcome
Can guide employees through procedures, summarize customer issues, and prepare response drafts.
In catalog06 · Cross-industry · Policy & compliance

Policy & Compliance Assistant

Scope
Allows employees to ask questions across internal policies, procedures, regulatory guidance, and compliance documents.
In AIRA
Finds relevant clauses, explains requirements, and provides references to the underlying approved source.
Outcome
Helps employees understand what is permitted, required, or needs escalation before taking an action.
Demo estate07 · Banking · Transaction risk

Fraud & Transaction Risk Assistant

Scope
Analyzes suspicious transactions, behavioral patterns, transaction history, and fraud alerts.
In AIRA
Summarizes anomalies, related accounts, unusual activity, and possible risk indicators for investigators.
Outcome
Supports case prioritization and investigation without replacing the bank's fraud-detection rules or models.
Pattern08 · Cross-industry · Customer relationships

Customer Insights Assistant

Scope
Analyzes customer activity, products, balances, transactions, behavior, and relationship information.
In AIRA
Creates customer summaries, segments, opportunity indicators, and potential service or retention insights.
Outcome
Helps relationship managers understand the customer faster and prepare for interactions.
Pattern09 · Banking · Regulatory reporting

Regulatory Reporting Assistant

Scope
Supports preparation and review of regulatory reports using approved banking data and reporting rules.
In AIRA
Checks data completeness, explains variances, summarizes exceptions, and assists with report commentary.
Outcome
Helps reporting teams investigate issues and prepare submissions with clearer audit trails.
Pattern10 · Cross-industry · Document processing

Document Intelligence Assistant

Scope
Processes documents such as statements, contracts, forms, invoices, financial statements, and applications across banking and other industries.
In AIRA
Extracts structured data, summarizes content, compares documents, and identifies important clauses or inconsistencies.
Outcome
Provides reusable document intelligence that other AIRA AI Apps can consume.
In catalog11 · Cross-industry · HR

Talent Match

Problem
Recruiters screen CVs inconsistently against a job description.
In AIRA
Upload candidate CVs and the job description, optionally with a candidate knowledge base, and receive a scored shortlist with reasons.
Controls
Reviewable reasons, the human decision retained, PII guardrails, entitlement-limited access.
Studio templates12 · Cross-industry

Business correspondence & summaries

Problem
Staff draft the same memos, status reports and customer emails every week.
In AIRA
Studio's templates (meeting summary, customer email, business memo, status report) become governed assistants in Home.
Controls
Models chosen by policy. DLP before and after the completion. Usage and spend attributed per user.
Pattern13 · Cross-industry · Procurement

Contract renewal watch

Problem
Auto-renewal and notice clauses are missed across hundreds of supplier contracts.
In AIRA
A scheduled Monitor AI Solution reads the contract repository and flags clauses approaching their notice dates; owners get a Work item and an alert.
Controls
Scheduled trigger through Runtime, read-only sources, alerts only through the channel door.
Pattern14 · Government

Citizen correspondence triage

Problem
Agencies receive large volumes of letters and emails that need routing and a compliant reply.
In AIRA
An email trigger starts a workflow that classifies each item, finds the responsible unit and drafts a reply grounded in published policy, held for an officer's approval.
Controls
Air-gapped with local models, approval before sending, a complete trail for freedom-of-information requests.
Pattern15 · Cross-industry · Regulatory change

Regulatory change monitoring

Problem
Compliance teams track new circulars by hand and map them to internal policy late.
In AIRA
A Monitor AI Solution ingests new publications, compares them with your policy library and opens a Work item listing the affected policies.
Controls
Cited comparisons, the policy owner's decision on the record, evidence exportable to audit.

Catalog and demo-estate items run in the AIRA demo estate with sample data; adapting them to your rules, documents and systems is part of a pilot. Patterns are illustrative designs built from standard AIRA capabilities. Outcomes are measured against each pilot's own baseline.

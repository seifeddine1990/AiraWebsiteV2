The problem

AI is already at work in your organisation. Nobody can prove what it did.

01 · Shadow AI

Data leaves by the clipboard

People paste customer data into public chatbots because there is no sanctioned alternative that is as good.

02 · No record

Nobody can answer the auditor

Who asked what, which model answered, what it cost and what it was allowed to see. The answer is scattered or missing.

03 · Sprawl

Every team, its own vendor

Separate contracts, separate keys, spend nobody can total and a new lock-in every quarter.

04 · Stalled pilots

Promising, then parked

Good use cases never reach production, because risk and audit cannot sign off on something they cannot inspect.

Six products

Consume. Create. Govern. Execute. Observe. Connect.

Every product is independently accessible, with its own roles and entitlements. Together they cover the whole life of enterprise AI, from the first question an employee asks to the evidence an examiner requests.

{{hex:hub}}Consume

AIRA Home

Where every employee uses AI. Ask for answers; Work for objectives. One inbox for approvals, running and failed work, with AI Solutions a click away.

  • Ask: governed chat across approved models, grounded in knowledge, with citations and abstention
  • Work: give AIRA an objective and follow it through approvals to a result
  • Knowledge Hub: access curated enterprise knowledge collections, from policies to compliance guidance, with collection-level permissions and answers grounded in source material
  • Document Insights: upload, OCR and chat with a document
  • The AI Solution catalog, with a run page for each type and replayable run history
{{hex:studio}}Create

AIRA Studio

Where teams build. One path from idea to production: Build → Configure → Assign AI → Test → Evaluate → Publish. What a builder tests in Studio is exactly what Runtime runs.

  • Four kinds of capability: AI Assistant, AI Agent, AI Flow and AI Solution
  • A Solution Advisor that drafts a capability from a plain-language description
  • Typed-node workflow canvas: prompt, knowledge, HTTP, code, database, agent, MCP tool, branching, error handling and subflows
  • Prompt Lab, a run debugger, and evaluation scenario libraries graded by programmatic checks plus an LLM judge
  • Templates, data sources, an encrypted connections vault and MCP servers
{{hex:gateway}}Unified access

AIRA Gateway

One controlled, audited entry point for on-premises and cloud LLMs, agents and AI Solutions. AIRA Nexus Runtime and external applications access the same governed catalog through application subscriptions.

  • One catalog: approved LLMs, agents and AI Solutions for Nexus and external applications
  • Application subscriptions: subscribe to catalog offerings with controlled access grants and limits
  • Policy controls: models, fallbacks, routing, guardrails, data policy and security level
  • Audited access: identity-bound requests, usage tracking and a record of every call
{{hex:runtime}}Execute

AIRA Runtime

Where published capabilities run. Runtime deploys versions, governs execution and gives your team full control over environments on Kubernetes and OpenShift. Access to models, agents and AI Solutions is controlled through AIRA Gateway.

  • Environment control: manage environments, versioned deployments and schedules on Kubernetes and OpenShift
  • Governed access: each deployed capability uses its own identity and access grants through Gateway
  • Execution governance: approvals, run history and a durable record of every execution
  • Triggers in and deliveries out with retry; usage and cost per deployment
  • Tenant isolation enforced in the database
{{hex:signal}}Observe

AIRA Signal

One answer to "what is our AI doing?" Signal observes runs, cost and health across the estate, and gives investigators the thread to pull when something looks wrong.

  • Overview of AI Solution runs, failure rate, node latency and spend, from one hour to ninety days
  • Activity, LLM activity, journeys, flagged events and incidents
  • Health of AI Solutions, knowledge, the workflow engine, the platform and the estate
  • Cost, spend and security views, with export
{{hex:data}}Connect

AIRA Data

The governed data plane. Connect enterprise systems, ingest documents exactly once, and keep every extraction reviewable against the content it came from.

  • Governed connectivity to PostgreSQL, SQL Server, MySQL, Oracle and IBM Db2, read-only by default, every query audited
  • Idempotent ingestion jobs that report incomplete attempts and never create duplicates
  • Document review bound to the content hash and the extractor version
  • A bounded tabular extraction service for CSV, TSV and JSON
{{hex:console}}

AIRA Console administers Nexus itself: identity (users, groups and service accounts in one table), organisations, product entitlements and roles, security (SSO, MFA, sessions), governance configuration, licensing, platform health with evidence, and the audit trail with chain verification and streaming export.

Architecture

One gateway. Every interaction governed.

AIRA Nexus brings the platform together around AIRA Gateway. AIRA Home users and external applications connect through this single entry point to approved LLMs and to agents and AI Solutions executed by AIRA Runtime. Every request follows the same access policies, limits and audit controls.

{{doors}}

Swap vendors, not code

Applications call a governed AI Endpoint, so the model behind it can change without a release.

Keys stay in one place

Provider keys are held by the Gateway, never by applications, and every call is policy-checked first.

Declared, never guessed

A local model server is explicit configuration. Declaring it opens exactly that exception, and nothing else.

Tamper-evident by construction

Evidence your auditors can verify, not just read.

Runs, approvals, policy decisions and administrative actions are written to a hash-chained log. A database-level trigger blocks updates and deletes, so the record cannot be quietly rewritten. The chain can be verified on demand and streamed to your SIEM or GRC tools.

Try it: edit an entry and watch verification fail at exactly that point. The hashes on the right are real SHA-256, computed in your browser.

Technical specifications

Built to be operated by your team.

Everything runs on your infrastructure. No telemetry and no phone-home. All images run as non-root.

Deployment
On-premises, private cloud or fully air-gapped. Kubernetes with base and overlays, plus an OpenShift overlay. Hostname-routed ingress with TLS at your edge.
Installer
aira-install provisions, builds and deploys, with a dry run that shows where every setting came from. aira seeds, starts, stops, diagnoses, backs up and upgrades.
Identity
Keycloak SSO over OIDC and SAML across every product, native TOTP MFA, JWTs with revocation, refresh rotation and an RS256 option.
Isolation
PostgreSQL row-level security enrolled and forced on tenant tables. Services connect as a role that cannot bypass it.
Cloud models
OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Mistral and Cohere pre-registered; any OpenAI-compatible endpoint can be added. Optional and customer-keyed.
Local models
Ollama on CPU or GPU, vLLM and SGLang on GPU, on your hardware. Declared in configuration, never auto-detected.
Routing
Aliases by cost, latency, quality or priority. Fallback chains, circuit state, benchmarks and a model playground.
Knowledge
Haystack pipelines, Qdrant vectors, FlashRank re-ranking, OCR and Office conversion at ingest, cited answers behind a grounding gate.
Workflows
Durable execution on Temporal, plus an inline engine for simple AI Solutions. A record for every node, with version pinning or late binding.
Agents
Conversational, job and reactive agents with tools and sub-agent trees under a hard budget for cost, time, LLM calls and depth.
Data stores
PostgreSQL as the source of truth, with Redis, Qdrant, MinIO and Temporal. All self-hosted.
Operations
Optional Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, Tempo and Alertmanager overlay. A health doctor that shows its evidence. Backup, restore-verify and a documented restore drill.

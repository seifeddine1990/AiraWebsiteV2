About AIRA Fusion

Artificial Intelligence Research & Application.

AIRA Fusion helps regulated organisations adopt AI securely, responsibly and at scale. We combine research with practical application through AIRA Nexus, a cloud-native platform for governing, developing, deploying and operating AI across cloud, on-premises and hybrid environments.

Secure•Regulated•Reliable
Why we exist

The models were the easy part.

Capable organisations kept stalling in the same place: not for lack of AI, but for lack of control over it. Knowing what was running, what it cost, what it was allowed to see and whether a regulator would accept it was where everything broke down.

We do not think the answer is one more tool. The answer is the layer underneath all of them: one governed plane to build any AI capability, run it on any model, observe it from one place and govern every call before it happens. Vendor-neutral, and running wherever you do.

Principles

What we will not trade away.

{{icon:shield}}

Governed by default

Control is not a setting someone remembers to turn on. Policy fires before the call, a trace records it during, and a tamper-evident trail proves it after, out of the box.

{{icon:key}}

Your data, your keys

You own the models, the keys and the deployment. The platform adapts to your perimeter, not the other way around, and it keeps running regardless of ours.

{{icon:check}}

Honest about stage

We state what is in place and what is not, claim no certification before we hold it, and would rather prove the platform on one real workload than oversell a roadmap.

{{icon:route}}

Neutral by design

The Gateway treats every model vendor the same. Swapping one for another is configuration, not a rewrite, so you never trade one lock-in for another.

{{icon:terminal}}

Built to be operated

An installer that explains every setting, a health doctor that shows its evidence, and runbooks your own team can own.

{{icon:spark}}

Research, applied

We track the frontier so you do not have to, and bring into the platform only what holds up under a regulated organisation's scrutiny.

Who we serve

Banks, governments and regulated enterprises.

Organisations operating in complex environments, where AI has to be practical, reusable and deployable with confidence, and where oversight, consistent controls and infrastructure choice are requirements rather than preferences.

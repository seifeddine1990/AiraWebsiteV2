Pilot programme

Prove it on one workload. Then scale it.

A pilot is a production deployment on your infrastructure, not a sandbox. We agree one narrow, measurable use case and write down the success criteria before we start. Your security team reviews the real system while it runs.

Scope a pilot →Browse use cases
How it works

Three phases, each with a decision at the end.

Scope

A workshop with your business owner, security and risk. We score two or three candidate use cases, pick one, and write the success criteria, the data needed and the controls to demonstrate.

Output: signed pilot plan

Deploy & run

AIRA Nexus is installed inside your perimeter on your models. The AI Solution is configured on your rules and documents and used by real people, with approval gates where decisions matter.

Output: working system, run records

Measure & decide

We measure against the baseline you recorded at the start, walk your auditors through the evidence trail, and decide together whether to expand, adjust or stop.

Output: results against criteria
Pilot Fit Scorer

The best pilots are narrow, measurable and owned.

Name up to three candidate use cases and score each from one to five against the six criteria. Start with the highest total, and bring the shortlist to the scoping workshop.

  1. Clear ownerA named business owner who will use the result and sign off the criteria.
    Clear owner
  2. Measurable todayYou already know the handling time, backlog, error rate or cost.
    Measurable today
  3. Available dataThe documents or records can be made available in the pilot environment.
    Available data
  4. Contained riskA person reviews the output: decision support, not an automated decision.
    Contained risk
  5. Reusable patternSuccess unlocks similar processes elsewhere in the organisation.
    Reusable pattern
  6. Governance visibilityRisk and audit can watch the controls work, which builds the case to scale.
    Governance visibility

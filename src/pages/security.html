{{icon:server}}

Your infrastructure

Run on-premises, in a private cloud or air-gapped. Data stores are self-hosted; cloud models are optional and use your keys.

{{icon:id}}

Identity & isolation

Single sign-on, MFA and server-side access decisions, with tenant isolation enforced in the database.

{{icon:filter}}

Policy before every call

Gateway applies approved models, guardrails, data policies and budgets. Approval gates keep people involved in consequential actions.

{{icon:chain}}

Evidence you can verify

Runs, approvals and policy decisions are recorded in a hash-chained audit log. Database controls prevent ordinary edits and deletes; verify and export the record for review.

    Who can use which model or AI solution?
  2. 02
